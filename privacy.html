Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What Odin is

Odin is a private, invite-only discovery network. You save places, services and things worth remembering, and find what the people you know have saved. We do not show you content from strangers, ads or algorithms.

What we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address and profile photo, via Google Sign-In. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Content you add — titles, descriptions, links, addresses, photos, and personal notes ("The Word").
  • Your connections — who you are connected with, invite and share links you create, and who joined through them.
  • Searches — the words you search for (typed or spoken), the results shown, and which results you open. We keep these to improve search.
  • Location — only when you allow it, for "near me" and distance features. When a search is logged, we keep only an approximate area (rounded to about 1 km), never your exact location.
  • Usage data — which items you save and view, when you were last active, and how many requests you make per hour (used to protect Odin from abuse).
  • Technical data — standard logs such as browser type and sign-in sessions, kept by our service providers.

Who can see what

This is Odin's core privacy rule. Please read it before adding anything.

When you add an item set to "Friends": your direct connections see it, with your name and your note.

If one of your connections saves it: their own connections can see the item, including your note, shown as "via" your connection. They do not see your name.

In plain terms: what you save can travel one step past your circle. Who you are does not.

"Only me" items are never shown to anyone else.

Invite links, share links and Founding Members

  • Invite links: when someone joins through your invite link, you are connected to each other automatically. Each link works for one person and expires after 30 days.
  • Share links: when you share an item, anyone who opens the link can see the item, its photo, your note and your first name, even without an account. Chat apps such as WhatsApp may show the item's name and photo in the message preview. A share link can be forwarded, so only share notes you are happy for others to read.
  • Founding Members: every new member is connected to the Founding Members account, so Odin is useful from day one. It is run by Odin's founder. Like any connection, it can see items you set to "Friends". You can remove it from your circle at any time.

Services we use

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Vercel — hosts the Odin website.
  • n8n — runs our search, add and translation processes.
  • OpenAI — understands searches, turns voice into text, translates, and creates search "embeddings" (a numeric summary) from your titles, descriptions and notes. OpenAI states that it does not use data sent through its API to train its models.
  • Google — sign-in, place details, addresses and nearby places.
  • Mapbox — address suggestions when you add a place.

Some of these services store or process data outside New Zealand, including Singapore (where our database is) and the United States. By using Odin, you agree to this.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not share your data with anyone other than the services listed above, unless the law requires it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile. This removes your account, the items you added, your notes, your saves and your connections. Copies in our providers' backups are removed as those backups expire.

Your rights (NZ Privacy Act 2020)

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Email odin.privacy@gmail.com and we will respond within 20 working days.

Changes to this policy

We will tell you about important changes in the app before they take effect.
